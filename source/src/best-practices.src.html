What has been tested, and what has not

AreaStatusHow
The bridge's behaviourTested45 automated tests, including negative tests, against a stand-in database. 19 deliberate bugs were all caught by a test.
Real database, one personTestedPing, hello, reading typed-but-unsubmitted text, disabled click, bad selector, invalid command, wait timeout, expired command, select and check, screenshot.
Private per-person channelTestedThe me path resolved to the owner's id and the lease worked on the real database. Another person's access has not been tried.
Closed page reopened by ClaudeWorks, slowlyThe first open request did nothing for about 100 seconds. After showing the artifact pane and opening again, the page loaded in about 25 seconds and ran the waiting command. Keep "open it yourself" as the fallback.
Opening another artifactTestedThe page stayed loaded and visible.
Two different people at onceNot testedFollows from the private-channel design. Try it with a second person.
Framework-controlled inputs (React and similar)Not testedThe bridge uses the browser's native setter, which is the standard approach. Test it on your own page.
Very large apps and long pagesNot testedReads are capped. Use selectors to read less.
