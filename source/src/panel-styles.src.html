Panel style gallery

Six looks for the remote-control panel. Each preview is live: it plays a made-up session (a command running, a second agent being turned away, a pause, a release, an error) so you can see every state. Pick one, copy its CSS, and replace the contents of rc-panel.css. The markup and the wiring do not change.

Preview on a

Use one

  1. Press Copy CSS on the style you like.
  2. Single-file app: replace the #rc rules in your <style> block. Multi-file app: replace the contents of rc/rc-panel.css.
  3. Done. The files are also in kit/styles/.

Make your own: keep the markup, keep the few lines at the bottom of each file under "structure every style keeps" (collapse and scrolling log), and change colours, spacing and fonts. The data-s attributes on #rc, #rc-state, #rc-lock and the log lines' data-k are your hooks.
